Privacy Policy

Tatal for Information Technology — operator of SEEN (seen.live) and Nvolve (nvolve.io), Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Last updated: November 8, 2026.

1. Scope

This policy explains how we handle information when you contact SEEN or Nvolve through WhatsApp or other messaging channels operated by us.

2. Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • The content of messages, images and files you send us.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, company, city and business needs.
  • Technical message metadata (timestamps and delivery status).

3. How we use it

  • To answer your questions about our products and services.
  • To prepare offers and connect you with our sales team.
  • To follow up on your request and improve our service.

Some replies are generated with the help of automated assistants (AI). You can ask to speak with a member of our team at any time.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to run the service — Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) for message delivery and AI service providers that process message text to help generate replies — and when required by law.

5. Retention and security

We keep conversation data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete it. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it.

6. Your rights

In line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you may ask us to stop messaging you at any time by replying "stop".

7. Data deletion

To delete your data, email hello@seen.live or hello@nvolve.io with your WhatsApp number, or send "delete my data" in the chat. We will process the request within 30 days.

8. Contact

Tatal for Information Technology, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — hello@seen.live
